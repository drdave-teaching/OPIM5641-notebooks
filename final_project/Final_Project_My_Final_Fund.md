# OPIM 5641 Final Project: My Final Fund

**Due Monday, December 7, 11:59 PM** (upload to HuskyCT). **Individual.** No group work and no presentations.
30% of your course grade.

## The short version

You build one final fund, let it run in the class race from Nov 23 to Dec 2, and write two pages about it.

| Step | When | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 · Studio 6 | Wed Nov 18, in class | pick at least 10 of the 100 biggest S&P 500 companies, solve Ms. Womack's model with your rules, and send your final fund to the Final Project leaderboard. You can change it and send again until the race starts. |
| 2 · The race | Mon Nov 23, 9:30 AM (the opening bell) to the Wed Dec 2 close | every fund sent before the opening bell starts together, with $10,000 at that morning's opening prices; watch your fund on the leaderboard; a change sent after the bell counts from the next market close |
| 3 · The report | due Mon Dec 7, 11:59 PM | two pages: what you built, why, what happened, and what you learned |

## What you hand in (two files)

1. **A 2-page report** (PDF) with the four parts below.
2. **Your Studio 6 notebook** (.ipynb), as you ran it for the fund that finished the race.

## The report (2 pages, four parts)

| Part | Length | What goes in it |
|---|---|---|
| 1 · My strategy and my picks | ½ page | which stocks you picked from the 100 and why, in your own words; back it up with numbers from the quilt (since Nov 5, 2026 so far, 3 years, average monthly return and monthly swing over 5 years and 1 year) |
| 2 · My model | ½ page | your settings (history, cap, floor, fewest stocks, risk limit) and why; your efficient frontier and where your fund sits on it; what the model bought and what it left out; what your rules cost next to the no-rules fund. Include the frontier and allocation pictures. |
| 3 · What happened | ½ page | Nov 23 to Dec 2: your fund next to the S&P 500, the class fund and the rest of the class (your rank); which stocks added and lost the most dollars; the news that explains it; any change you made along the way and why |
| 4 · What I learned | ½ page | what the model got right and wrong (it read the past; the race was the future); one thing you would change; would you hold this fund into 2027, and why |

## Rubric (100 points)

| Row | Points | Excellent | Good | Needs work |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 · My strategy and my picks | 20 | A clear strategy in your own words; each group of picks has a reason tied to numbers from the quilt | Picks and a strategy are stated; the reasons are thin or have no numbers | A list of tickers with no reasons |
| 2 · My model | 25 | Every setting explained in plain words; reads the frontier correctly (risk across, return up) and says where the fund sits and why; names what the model left out and what the rules cost | Settings and the fund are reported; the frontier or the cost of the rules is missing | Settings listed with no explanation; no frontier |
| 3 · What happened | 25 | Final value, return and rank next to the S&P 500 and the class fund, with numbers; names the stocks that drove the result, in dollars; connects at least one to the news; explains any change | Results reported with numbers; the why is thin | No comparison, or no numbers |
| 4 · What I learned | 15 | Separates what was the model, what was the picks and what was luck; one specific change; a clear yes or no on holding into 2027 that follows from the evidence | Some reflection; the recommendation does not follow from the evidence | A summary with no reflection |
| 5 · Clear and professional | 15 | Two pages; figures labeled and readable; every number can be found in the notebook or on the leaderboard | Mostly clear, with small gaps | Over length, unlabeled figures, or numbers with no source |

**Your grade does not depend on making money.** It depends on how clearly you explain your choices and what happened.

## The race (for fun; not part of the grade)

The fund worth the most at the Wednesday, Dec 2 close wins the class race. Dr. Dave will write the winner a
recommendation on their LinkedIn profile.

## Questions you may have

* **When does the race start?** At the opening bell on Monday, Nov 23 (9:30 AM Eastern). Every final fund sent before
  then starts together, with $10,000 at that morning's opening prices.
* **Can I change my final fund after tonight?** Yes. Open the Studio 6 notebook, Run all, change your picks or
  settings, and send again. Before the opening bell on Nov 23, the last fund you send is the one that starts the
  race. After the bell, a change counts from the next market close. Every send is saved with its date and your
  reason. Say what you changed in Part 3 of the report.
* **What are the rules for a final fund?** At least 10 stocks, at most 25% in any one, all from the 100 biggest S&P
  500 companies.
* **Do I need to make money to get a good grade?** No. See the line under the rubric.
* **Can I use Claude or another AI tool?** Yes, for help with code, for reading the news PDF, and for explaining what
  you see. The numbers must come from your notebook or the leaderboard, and the reasons in your report must be yours.
* **Where do I get my final numbers?** On the Final Project leaderboard after the Dec 2 close: your row and your
  fund's page. The same table has two rows that are not ranked: the S&P 500 and the class fund.
* **What is the class fund?** The average of the class's Studio 5 funds: what you would own if you put the same
  amount in every classmate's fund. On the Final Project leaderboard it starts with $10,000 at the same opening bell as
  the final funds, so you can compare it with yours over the same days.
* **The syllabus says group project.** The final project is individual this semester. This page replaces the "Group
  Projects" paragraph in the syllabus.
